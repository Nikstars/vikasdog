About us

We raise guardians with character and heart

Kennel Vikas-Dog is a family kennel in the village of Topolovo, Plovdiv region. We have been FCI registered since 2013 and are devoted to a single breed – the Caucasian Shepherd.

The founder of the kennel is the president of the National Club “Caucasian Shepherd – Bulgaria”. We work side by side with specialists, judges and breeders and follow the breed standard closely.

Our dogs live outdoors in a family environment, grow up around people and animals and are regularly shown. This way every puppy gets good health, correct type and a balanced temperament.
