How it works

Four steps to your puppy

  1. Enquiry

    Fill in the form below and receive a confirmation by email.

  2. Conversation

    We discuss what dog you are looking for and tell you about the parents of the next litter.

  3. Visit

    You come to the kennel in Topolovo and meet our dogs.

  4. Welcome home

    When the litter is old enough, you choose your puppy and it leaves for its new home.

What you get

Every puppy leaves with

  • Pedigree
  • Pet passport
  • Microchip
  • Age-appropriate vaccinations
  • Deworming
  • Sales contract
  • Feeding and training advice
  • Our support for its whole life
