Club Show 2026

Specialized Club Show of the Caucasian Shepherd · 18 October 2026 · Maritsa Park, Dimitrovgrad

About the show

A celebration of the breed

On 18 October 2026 Maritsa Park in Dimitrovgrad hosted the Specialized Club Show of the Caucasian Shepherd, organised by the National Club “Caucasian Shepherd – Bulgaria” under the patronage of the Bulgarian National Cynological Federation.

Dogs from different kennels were judged by Marian Draganescu. The day ended with the award ceremony, trophies and gifts from the sponsors.

  • Date 18 October 2026
  • Venue Maritsa Park, Dimitrovgrad
  • Organiser National Club “Caucasian Shepherd – Bulgaria”
  • Judge Marian Draganescu
