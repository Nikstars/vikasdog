The dogs that live with us

This page shows only our own dogs. No show photos of other people's dogs and no other kennels.

Our males stand over 80 cm at the withers, and the biggest is 90 cm tall and weighs 100 kg. All of them grew up in a family setting – calm with their own people and serious guardians towards strangers.
