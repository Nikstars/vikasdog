We raise guardians with character and heart

Kennel Vikas-Dog is a family kennel in the village of Topolovo, Plovdiv region, devoted to a single breed: the Caucasian Shepherd.

Our dogs live outdoors in a family environment, grow up around people and animals and are regularly shown. This way every puppy gets good health, correct type and a balanced temperament.

Winter is their season

The Caucasian Shepherd comes from the mountains, and its thick coat keeps it warm in the harshest cold. When the snow piles up, our dogs are in no hurry to come inside. That is when they are happiest.

Our dogs at shows

Every dog we breed from holds the highest show grade, Excellent, and a show title.
